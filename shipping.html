Shipping & Returns

Shipping

Orders are typically processed within 1–2 business days.

Shipping timelines may vary depending on location, inventory, and fulfillment timing.

At this time, ROCK Water ships within the United States.

Returns

If there is an issue with your order, please contact us and we'll do our best to help.

Because beverages are consumable products, opened items are not eligible for return.

Damaged or incorrect orders may be replaced or refunded when appropriate.
