1% for the Planet

1% of our sales are donated to support impactful environmental solutions worldwide.

What It Means

1% for the Planet is a global movement that connects businesses and individuals with environmental organizations making a measurable difference. Through this commitment, companies pledge to donate 1% of sales to support vetted environmental partners around the world.

Why It Matters

Environmental challenges affect the landscapes, waterways, and outdoor spaces that inspire everyday life. Supporting long-term solutions helps protect the natural systems that communities, wildlife, and future generations depend on.

Our Commitment

At ROCK Water, this commitment reflects our belief that business should give back to the places that give us so much. By contributing 1% of sales, we aim to support organizations working to preserve ecosystems, protect clean water, and strengthen environmental resilience.

Looking Ahead

As ROCK Water grows, so does our responsibility. Our goal is to keep building a brand that supports a healthier relationship between people, water, and the outdoors.
