Data Protection

Text Messaging (SMS) Program and Mobile Information

If you opt in to ROCK text messages (for example through a signup form on our website or at checkout), we will send you text messages regarding our products, orders, updates, policies, and promotional offers. You can opt out at any time.

By subscribing to our SMS program, you consent to receive SMS messages at the phone number provided. Message frequency may vary based on your interaction with our service. Message and data rates may apply. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

We do not share your mobile information with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Additionally, all the categories of personal data described in this policy exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent. This information will not be shared with any third parties under any circumstances. However, we may share your data with trusted service providers who assist us in operating our SMS program (such as our email and text messaging platform), provided they agree to keep your data confidential and use it solely for the purpose of providing services on our behalf.

Opt-out option: you may opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying "STOP" to any of our messages, or reply "HELP" for help. You can also contact us at support@rockbeverage.com. Opting out will not affect your other interactions with our services. You can opt out of marketing emails with the "unsubscribe" link in any email.

Cookies and Service Providers

We use service providers to run our store and communications, such as our website host, payment and checkout providers, and our email and text messaging platform (Klaviyo), which may use cookies to understand how visitors use our site. We do not sell your personal information. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, verify, or remove your personal information at any time. To manage your information, contact support@rockbeverage.com.
