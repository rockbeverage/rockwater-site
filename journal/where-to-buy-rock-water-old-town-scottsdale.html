Where to Buy ROCK Water in Old Town Scottsdale

Scottsdale, Arizona · October 21, 2026 · By Brett Yanofsky

Three Old Town Scottsdale spots carry ROCK: Juice Core Organic, Kill Club and Not My First Rodeo. Here is where to find a cold can and how to plan the walk.

If you are wondering where to buy ROCK water in Scottsdale, Old Town is the easiest place to start. Three local spots within a short walk of each other carry ROCK: Juice Core Organic and Kill Club on Stetson Drive, and Not My First Rodeo on 6th Avenue. Each one is a few minutes from the next, so you can grab a cold can wherever your afternoon takes you.

The three Old Town spots

Here is the short list, with addresses you can drop straight into your maps app.

  • Kill Club, 7318 E Stetson Dr, Scottsdale 85251
  • Not My First Rodeo, 7150 E 6th Ave Suite B, Scottsdale 85251

A simple way to walk it

Old Town Scottsdale is built for walking, especially in patio season, which runs from fall through spring. The three spots sit close enough together that you do not need a plan so much as a direction.

  • Start on Stetson Drive. Juice Core Organic and Kill Club are both on the same street, so you can hit one and keep moving.
  • Head toward 6th Avenue. Not My First Rodeo is a short walk away, a good stop on the way back to the car.
  • Keep a can in hand. Old Town is a place where you end up wandering longer than you meant to. A cold drink makes that easier.

If you are in town for Scottsdale ArtWalk, the same idea applies. Park once, walk the galleries, and stop in where it fits.
