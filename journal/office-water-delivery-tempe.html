Office Water Delivery in Tempe: Monthly Cases That Run Themselves

Tempe, Arizona · November 9, 2026 · By Brett Yanofsky

Office water delivery in Tempe without the jugs: canned spring water by the case, free monthly delivery, and a schedule you can skip or pause anytime.

Office water delivery in Tempe usually means one of two things: a jug cooler in the corner or somebody hauling cases of plastic bottles in from the parking lot. There is a simpler way. Pick a number of cases, set a monthly schedule, and let the water show up on its own. That is how ROCK office delivery works for teams in Tempe and across the Phoenix area, and it takes the whole job off someone's list.

The problem with keeping an office stocked

Every office has a person who quietly ends up in charge of the water. They notice the fridge is low, place an order, wait for it, and carry it in. It is a small job until it falls through the cracks during a busy week and the team is drinking from the sink.

Teams in Tempe feel this more than most. Plenty of offices sit around Downtown Tempe and Mill Avenue, near the lake, or out along the edges of Mesa and Chandler. People walk to lunch, come back in from site visits, or ride in from campus. Arizona is hot and dry for a good part of the year, and the first thing anyone wants when they get back to their desk is something cold to drink.

How office water delivery in Tempe works with ROCK

The setup takes a few minutes and then it runs in the background.

  • Pick your cases. ROCK comes in 12-pack cases of 12oz aluminum cans.
  • Choose monthly or one time. A monthly subscription comes with free monthly delivery. A one-time order works for a launch party or an offsite.
  • Adjust when you need to. Skip a month or pause anytime. Holiday weeks and slow seasons happen.

Pricing is by the case. 1 case is $19.68, which is $1.64 a can. 2 cases are $38.40, or $1.60 a can. At 4 or more cases it drops to $18 a case, which is $1.50 a can, so 4 cases come to $72 for 48 cans. For most small teams, that is a simple monthly number that does not need a meeting to approve.

Why cans instead of jugs or plastic bottles

Jug coolers need a spot on the floor, a delivery person to swap them out, and cups. Cases of plastic bottles fill a recycling bin fast and rarely feel like something you want to set in front of a client. A can fixes most of that.

  • It stacks. Cans line up on a fridge shelf, so anyone can see when the supply is getting low.
  • It travels. Grab one for a drive to a meeting in Phoenix or a walk around Tempe Town Lake.
  • It recycles. Aluminum is infinitely recyclable. ROCK is never sold in plastic.
  • It looks intentional. A cold can on the conference table says somebody thought about the details.
