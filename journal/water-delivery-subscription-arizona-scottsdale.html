Water Delivery Subscription in Arizona: How Subscribe and Save Works

Scottsdale, Arizona · November 19, 2026 · By Brett Yanofsky

How the ROCK water delivery subscription works in Arizona: 30% off every 12-pack, deliveries every 2 weeks to 2 months, and skip, pause or cancel anytime.

If you want a water delivery subscription in Arizona that you set once and stop thinking about, here is how ROCK home delivery works. You pick how often you want a 12-pack, and you save 30% on every one. That brings a 12-pack from $34 to $23.80. Deliveries come every 2 weeks, every month or every 2 months, and you can skip, pause or cancel anytime. ROCK is a water brand based in Scottsdale, and we built subscribe and save for people who would rather not run out.

What you get in every box

Each delivery is a 12-pack of ROCK: natural spring water with minerals and electrolytes, 0 sugar, 0 calories, nothing artificial, in a 12oz aluminum can. Nothing about the water changes when you subscribe. Same can, same water, lower price.

We put it in aluminum because aluminum is infinitely recyclable, and we will never use plastic. If you want the longer version of that decision, read why we chose aluminum.

How the water delivery subscription works in Arizona

There are only a few choices to make, and you can change them later.

  • Pick subscribe and save. On the shop page, choose the subscription option instead of a one time order. A one time 12-pack is $34. With subscribe and save it is $23.80.
  • Pick a rhythm. Every 2 weeks, every month or every 2 months. Most people pick based on how fast the fridge empties.
  • Adjust when life changes. Skip a delivery when you travel. Pause it for a while. Cancel anytime. No phone call, no fine print.

That is the whole system. No points, no tiers, no countdown.

Which delivery rhythm fits you

There is no right answer, only the one that matches how you live. A few ways to think about it:

  • Every 2 weeks. A household in Scottsdale or Phoenix where ROCK goes in the gym bag, the car and the lunch bag. If cans disappear fast, this keeps the fridge stocked.
  • Every month. The middle option. A can with lunch, a can after a walk around Papago Park, one in the cooler on a weekend.
  • Every 2 months. For people who want ROCK on hand without it being the only thing in the fridge. A good fit if you mostly reach for a can on hikes or golf days.

Start with a guess. If you end up with extra cans, skip one. If you run out early, move to a shorter rhythm. The point is that the decision stays small.

Presale timing, said plainly

ROCK still water is on presale right now. Orders placed today ship in January 2027. That includes new subscriptions, so your first box arrives with that shipment, and the rhythm you picked takes over from there.

We would rather tell you that up front than let you assume a box is on its way this week. There is no pressure to order today. The price and the options are the same whenever you are ready.

Why we built it this way

Brett Yanofsky started ROCK because he could not find a water brand he actually connected with. Plastic felt off, glass did not make sense everywhere, and most canned options did not reflect how he lives. You can read the rest in the founder story.

Subscribe and save comes from the same thinking. Water is something you drink every day, so buying it should be simple and quiet. A subscription that is easy to skip or cancel is one we would use ourselves. As our north star puts it: "Nothing is more basic to the quality of life than the quality of the water you drink."

Every order also counts toward something bigger. 1% of sales go to environmental causes through 1% for the Planet, whether you buy one 12-pack or subscribe for the year.
