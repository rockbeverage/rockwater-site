Local Arizona Brands in Phoenix: Why We Deliver Ourselves

Phoenix, Arizona · November 11, 2026 · By Brett Yanofsky

Why local Arizona brands and the Phoenix businesses that carry them make the Valley feel like home, and why ROCK's founder still delivers cases himself.

Local Arizona brands are a big part of what makes Phoenix feel like home. The coffee shop that knows your order, the deli on the corner, the studio down the street, the market that stocks things made nearby. When you choose a local business, you are choosing the people who run it and the neighborhood around it. That is the reason ROCK is built the way it is, and the reason our founder, Brett Yanofsky, still delivers cases to local accounts himself.

What makes the Valley feel like home

Phoenix is a big metro. It can feel spread out, all freeways and parking lots, until you find your places. Then it shrinks. A few blocks in Arcadia or on Roosevelt Row start to feel like a small town. Downtown Tempe and Mill Avenue, Downtown Gilbert and Downtown Chandler each have their own version of the same thing: owners behind the counter, regulars at the same table, and a shared sense that this corner of the Valley belongs to the people in it.

We wrote about one of those moments in our guide to First Friday on Roosevelt Row. Walk those blocks on a busy night and you see it right away. Local businesses are not just places to buy things. They are where a city gets its character.

Why local Arizona brands matter

A local brand is not better just because it is local. It still has to be good. But when it is made or run by people who live here, a few things tend to follow.

  • You can reach a real person. If something is off, there is a name and a phone number, not a ticket queue.
  • It is built for here. Arizona summers are hot and dry. A brand from the Valley understands what a long afternoon outside actually feels like.
  • The relationship goes both ways. Local businesses carry local brands, and local brands send people back to those businesses.
  • It shows up in the community. At markets, at events, at the counter. Not just on a screen.

That last point is the one we care about most. A brand that only exists online is easy to make. A brand that people run into around town takes longer, and it means more.

Why we deliver ourselves

ROCK is based in Scottsdale. Brett founded it in July 2025 after realizing he could not find a water brand he actually connected with. Plastic felt off, glass did not make sense everywhere, and most canned options did not reflect how he lives. So he built one. You can read the full version on our story page.

From the start, ROCK has been founder-led. Brett personally sells to and delivers for local accounts across the Phoenix area. That is not a marketing line. It is how the first cases get from the warehouse to the cooler.

We do it this way for a few simple reasons:

  • We learn faster. Standing in a cafe or a gym while the cooler gets stocked tells us more than any report would.
  • Owners know who to call. Every account has Brett's number. Businesses can text him directly at 480-253-2829.
  • It keeps us honest. When the founder is the one carrying the case, the details matter.

Brett grew up here. He went to school in the Scottsdale Unified School District and studied finance at Arizona State University. The Valley is not a market we picked off a map. It is home, and delivering in person is how we stay close to it.

What ROCK brings to a local business

ROCK is natural spring water with minerals and electrolytes, 0 sugar, 0 calories and nothing artificial, in a 12oz aluminum can. Aluminum is infinitely recyclable, and we will never use plastic. Cans chill fast, stack clean in a cooler and look good on a counter.

"Nothing is more basic to the quality of life than the quality of the water you drink."

It fits the kinds of places that make the Valley feel like home: cafes, restaurants, gyms and fitness studios, wellness spaces, golf clubs, hotels, specialty markets, boutiques and offices. We covered the cafe side in more detail in wholesale water for cafes in Phoenix.
