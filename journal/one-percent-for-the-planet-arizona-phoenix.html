1% for the Planet in Arizona: What the ROCK Pledge Means

Phoenix, Arizona · November 5, 2026 · By Brett Yanofsky

ROCK, a Scottsdale water brand sold across Phoenix, gives 1% of sales to environmental causes through 1% for the Planet. Here is what that pledge means.

If you have seen the 1% for the Planet mark on an Arizona brand and wondered what it means, here is the plain version for ROCK. We give 1% of our sales to environmental causes through 1% for the Planet. Not 1% of profit in a good year. One percent of sales, every sale. ROCK is a water brand based in Scottsdale, and Brett Yanofsky sells to and delivers for accounts across Phoenix himself. The pledge is part of the brand from the start, not something we added later to look good.

What 1% for the Planet in Arizona means for ROCK

The idea is simple. When someone buys ROCK, a share of that sale goes to environmental causes. A 12-pack ordered for a home in Arcadia counts. A case delivered to an office in Tempe counts. A cooler stocked at a gym in Old Town Scottsdale counts.

We chose sales on purpose. A promise tied to profit can quietly shrink to nothing while a company grows. A promise tied to sales grows right along with the business. The more people across Phoenix and the rest of the Valley who drink ROCK, the more goes back.

You can read our own summary on the 1% for the Planet page, and the program itself explains how it works at onepercentfortheplanet.org.

Why a water brand should give back

Water does not come from nowhere. Every can of ROCK depends on the natural world working the way it should. It would feel strange to build a water company and take from that without giving anything back.

We have a line we come back to often: "Nothing is more basic to the quality of life than the quality of the water you drink." If we believe that, then caring about the places water comes from is part of the job. The pledge is how we make that belief show up in the numbers, not just in a caption.

It also fits how Brett lives. He started ROCK in July 2025 because he could not find a water brand he connected with. Health, time and connection matter to him, and so does being outside. A brand that reflects that should care about the outdoors it is built around.

The outdoors we think about in the Valley

Living in Maricopa County makes this feel close to home. So much of life here happens outside:

  • Morning climbs on Camelback Mountain and Piestewa Peak in Phoenix.
  • Long trail days at South Mountain Park and Preserve and the McDowell Sonoran Preserve.
  • Easy loops through Papago Park, where Phoenix and Tempe meet.
  • Time on the water at Tempe Town Lake and along the Salt River.

These are the places our customers spend their weekends. We wrote about one of them in our guide to what to bring for a Camelback morning climb. The pledge is our way of saying those places matter, here and everywhere else.

How the pledge connects to the can

Giving 1% of sales is one piece. The package is another. Every ROCK is natural spring water in a 12oz aluminum can, and aluminum is infinitely recyclable. We will never use plastic.

The two decisions came from the same place. If we are going to make something people drink every day, we want the whole thing to hold up, from what is inside the can to the can itself to what happens after a sale. We covered the can in more detail in why we chose aluminum.

We are not claiming one water brand changes the world on its own. We are saying a small brand can build good habits early and keep them as it grows. That is the point of making the pledge on day one instead of someday.

What it means when you buy ROCK

You do not have to do anything extra. There is no checkbox and no add-on. Buying ROCK is enough, because 1% of every sale goes to environmental causes through 1% for the Planet.

Here is what you get in the can: natural spring water with minerals and electrolytes, 0 sugar, 0 calories and nothing artificial.
