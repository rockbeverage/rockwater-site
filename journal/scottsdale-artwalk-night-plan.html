Scottsdale ArtWalk Night: A Simple Plan for Old Town

Scottsdale, Arizona · November 18, 2026 · By Brett Yanofsky

A simple plan for Scottsdale ArtWalk night in Old Town: when to arrive, how to walk the gallery blocks, what to carry, and how to end the evening well.

Scottsdale ArtWalk is the evening art walk in Old Town Scottsdale, when the galleries open their doors and people drift from block to block looking at new work. It is free, it is walkable, and there is no wrong way to do it. The simple plan: get to Old Town Scottsdale before dark, park once, walk the gallery blocks slowly, carry water, and save a little time at the end for a table outside. That is the whole night, and it is a good one.

What Scottsdale ArtWalk is like

Old Town has more galleries packed into a few walkable blocks than most cities have in total. Painting, sculpture, photography, jewelry, Western art, contemporary work. On ArtWalk nights, the doors stay open into the evening, the sidewalks fill, and the whole area takes on a slower, friendlier pace.

You do not need to know anything about art to enjoy it. Most people walk in, look around, ask a question or two, and walk out to the next door. Some come to buy. Most come to see what is new and to be outside on a good night. People come in from Paradise Valley, Fountain Hills, Tempe, Phoenix and the rest of the Valley for exactly that reason.

A simple plan for the night

Keep it loose. A few small choices make the evening easier.

  • Arrive in the last light. Getting there before sunset means easier parking and a chance to see the galleries before the busiest stretch of the evening.
  • Park once and walk. Old Town is built for it. Leave the car and let the blocks connect on foot.
  • Pick a loose route. Start at one end of the gallery blocks and work your way across. Do not try to see everything. Two or three places you really look at beat twelve you rush through.
  • Talk to people. Gallery staff and artists are usually happy to explain a piece. That is where the night gets interesting.
  • End outside. Finish with a seat on a patio or a bench and let the evening wind down.

If you like this kind of night, Downtown Phoenix has its own version. We wrote a guide to First Friday on Roosevelt Row that pairs well with this one.

What to carry on an ArtWalk night

You are on your feet for a few hours, mostly outside, in dry desert air. Pack light and think practical.

  • Comfortable shoes. Sidewalks, gallery floors and a lot of standing.
  • A light layer. Evenings in Scottsdale cool off quickly once the sun drops, especially from fall through spring.
  • Water. Dry air pulls moisture out quietly, even on a mild night. Drinking regularly is standard advice for anyone spending time outdoors in Arizona. For heat and weather guidance, check the National Weather Service in Phoenix.
  • A small bag. Hands free is better when you are walking in and out of doors.

Galleries are not the place for open drinks, so finish your water before you walk in or keep it sealed in your bag. A can that closes the conversation outside and goes in the recycling bin after is about as simple as it gets.

Where ArtWalk fits in Scottsdale's season

ArtWalk feels most at home in the cooler months, when patio season is in full swing and the whole city lives outside. It sits alongside the rest of what makes winter and spring busy here: Barrett-Jackson, the WM Phoenix Open at TPC Scottsdale, and spring training season. On the quieter nights in between, ArtWalk is the easy plan. No tickets, no schedule, just a walk.

Questions

What is Scottsdale ArtWalk?

Scottsdale ArtWalk is an evening art walk in Old Town Scottsdale where galleries open their doors and visitors walk between them to see new work. It is free and open to anyone.

Where should I park for Scottsdale ArtWalk?

The simplest plan is to park once in Old Town Scottsdale before sunset and walk the gallery blocks on foot. Arriving in the last light usually makes parking easier.

What should I bring to Scottsdale ArtWalk?

Bring comfortable shoes, a light layer for the cooler evening, a small bag and water, since you will be walking outside in dry desert air for a few hours.
