Tempe Town Lake Things to Do: Walk, Ride, Paddle

Tempe, Arizona · November 27, 2026 · By Brett Yanofsky

Looking for Tempe Town Lake things to do this weekend? Walk the path, ride a loop, get out on the water, and keep a cold can waiting in the cooler for after.

Looking for Tempe Town Lake things to do this weekend? Keep it simple: walk the path in the morning, ride a loop on two wheels, get out on the water if you want to paddle, and finish with something cold from the cooler. Tempe Town Lake sits on the Salt River right next to Downtown Tempe and Mill Avenue, a short drive from Phoenix, Scottsdale, Mesa and Chandler. It is one of the easiest places in the Valley to spend a whole Saturday outside without planning much at all.

Why Tempe Town Lake works for a weekend

Most outdoor plans in Arizona mean a trailhead, a climb and an early alarm. The lake is different. It is flat, open and close to everything. You can walk out of a coffee shop on Mill Avenue and be at the water in a few minutes.

That makes it a good pick for mixed groups. A friend who runs, a friend who wants to stroll, a kid on a scooter and a dog all fit the same morning. Fall through spring is patio season in the Valley, and that is when the lake feels easiest to enjoy of the year.

Walk the path in the morning

The paths around the lake are the easiest place to start. Go early. The light is low, the water is calm, and the path is quiet before the day fills in.

  • A short walk. Stay near Downtown Tempe and turn around whenever you want.
  • A longer loop. Cross a bridge and come back on the other shore for a full lap.
  • Add a climb. Papago Park is close by if you want rocks and a view after the flat part.

There is not a lot of shade along the water, so bring a hat and sunglasses even on a mild morning. Check the forecast from the National Weather Service in Phoenix before you head out.

Ride a loop on two wheels

A bike turns the lake into a bigger day. The paths are wide and mostly flat, and you can link the lake with rides along the Salt River or over toward Papago Park. It is an easy ride for anyone who has not been on a bike in a while.

Keep your speed friendly on busy weekend mornings. Walkers, runners and strollers share the same path. A bottle cage and a small bag for your phone and keys are all you really need.

Get out on the water

Seeing Tempe from the middle of the lake is a different kind of morning. If you plan to paddle, check the rules first. The City of Tempe posts lake information and updates, so look there before you load the car.

On the water, the sun reflects back up at you, so sunscreen matters more than it seems. Keep your water within reach, not buried in a bag you cannot open while you paddle.

Water and the cooler, kept simple

Arizona air is dry, and even an easy day outside takes more water than people expect. Carrying water and drinking regularly is standard advice for anyone outdoors here. Questions

What is there to do at Tempe Town Lake?

Walk or run the paths around the water, ride a bike loop, or get out on the lake to paddle. Downtown Tempe and Mill Avenue are right next to it for food after.

What time of day should you visit Tempe Town Lake?

Go early in the morning, because the path is quiet and it is the cooler part of the day. Fall through spring is the most comfortable stretch of the year to spend time outside in Tempe.

What should I bring to Tempe Town Lake?

Bring water, a hat, sunglasses and sunscreen, since there is little shade along the shore. A small cooler with ice and a few cans in the car makes the end of the day better.
