Golf Weekend in Scottsdale: What's in the Bag

Scottsdale, Arizona · November 13, 2026 · By Brett Yanofsky

Planning a golf weekend in Scottsdale? Here is what we pack beyond the clubs: cold water, sun cover, simple snacks and a plan for the back nine in dry desert air.

What should you pack for a golf weekend in Scottsdale? Beyond the clubs, the bag that works here carries more water than you think you need, real sun cover, a few simple snacks and a towel you can soak. The courses are green, but the air is desert air, and it stays dry most of the year. A round that starts cool at dawn can feel very different by the back nine. Here is the list we use, from the first tee time to the walk back to the car.

What's in the bag for a golf weekend in Scottsdale

Start with the basics and keep them in the same pocket every round, so nothing gets left in the trunk.

  • Water, and more of it. Fill the cooler pocket before you leave the house. On a full round in the sun, water runs out before anything else.
  • Sunscreen, a hat and sunglasses. Reapply at the turn. The Scottsdale sun is strong even on a cool fall morning.
  • A towel you can wet. One for the clubs, one for the back of your neck.
  • Simple snacks. A sandwich, fruit, nuts, something salty. Easy to eat between shots.
  • Extra balls and tees. The desert along the fairways keeps what it takes.
  • A light layer. Early tee times in patio season can start chilly and warm up fast.

Desert air and the back nine

Arizona summers are hot and dry, and even in the milder months the air pulls moisture out of you without much notice. Walking a course, or even riding in a cart, adds up over a few hours. Sweat carries out water and some electrolytes, so people who are active or outdoors in the heat often pay closer attention to both. Electrolytes such as sodium, potassium and magnesium are minerals the body uses to help maintain fluid balance. Our piece on electrolyte water in Phoenix explains it in plain terms, and simple habits for Arizona heat covers the everyday side.

The habit that works on the course is simple. Drink something on every tee box, not just when you feel thirsty. Refill at the turn. Before you book an afternoon round, check the forecast from the National Weather Service in Phoenix, and use the CDC heat and health pages for real heat safety information. We make water. We are not the ones to give medical advice.

Questions

What should I bring for a round of golf in Scottsdale?

Bring plenty of water, sunscreen, a hat, sunglasses, a towel you can wet, simple snacks and extra balls. Early tee times can start cool, so a light layer helps too.

When is the most comfortable time to golf in Scottsdale?

Early morning tee times are usually the most comfortable, especially in the warmer months. Fall through spring is patio season in Scottsdale, when many golfers plan their weekends.

How much water should I bring on a Scottsdale golf course?

Bring more than you think you need and refill at the turn, since the dry desert air makes it easy to lose water without noticing. On hot days, follow guidance from the National Weather Service Phoenix and the CDC heat and health pages.
