Sparkling Spring Water in Arizona: How the ROCK Preorder Works

Scottsdale, Arizona · November 12, 2026 · By Brett Yanofsky

ROCK Sparkling is coming. Here is how the first batch wholesale preorder works for Arizona businesses: the 10 case minimum, $1.50 a can and free shipping.

If you run a business and have been looking for sparkling spring water in Arizona, here is the short version: ROCK Sparkling is coming, and the first batch is open now as a wholesale preorder. ROCK is a water brand based in Scottsdale. Businesses reserve cases today at $1.50 a can, pay at order, and the cases ship free with the first sparkling batch. We do not have a ship date yet, and we will not guess at one. When it is set, everyone who preordered gets an email.

Why ROCK is adding sparkling

ROCK started as one simple product: natural spring water with minerals and electrolytes, 0 sugar, 0 calories, nothing artificial, in a 12oz aluminum can. That is still the core of the brand, and it is not changing.

Sparkling came from the people who already pour ROCK. Cafe owners, gym front desks and office managers across Scottsdale, Phoenix and Tempe kept asking the same question when Brett dropped off cases: is there a sparkling one? Some guests want bubbles with lunch. Some want something a little different with 0 sugar. A cooler with both still and sparkling covers more of the people who open it.

So we are building it the same way we built the still water. Same can. Same standards. Same rule that it has to feel like ROCK before it goes out the door. If you want the backstory on how we think about the package, read why we chose aluminum.

How the sparkling spring water preorder works in Arizona

The first batch is wholesale only. That means it is for businesses: cafes, restaurants, gyms and fitness studios, wellness spaces, golf clubs, hotels, specialty markets, boutiques and offices. Here is how it works:

  • 10 case minimum. Each case is a 12-pack of 12oz cans.
  • $1.50 a can. Same price as our still water at 4 or more cases.
  • Paid at order. You reserve your cases by paying when you place the preorder.
  • Ships free with the first batch. There is no shipping charge on the preorder.
  • Ship date by email. No ship date exists yet. When we have one, we email it to every account that preordered.

You can place a preorder on our wholesale page. If you would rather talk it through first, text Brett at 480-253-2829.

Why we are doing a preorder first

We would rather be honest about timing than promise something we cannot control. A first batch takes planning, and the preorder tells us how much to make for the businesses that want it. It also means the accounts that raised their hand early are the first ones with sparkling in the cooler.

There is no countdown here and no pressure. If you are not ready to commit to sparkling, still water is available on the same wholesale page. You can start there and add sparkling later.

Who sparkling fits

Think about where people in the Valley already reach for something with bubbles:

  • Cafes and restaurants. A sparkling option next to the still water on a patio in Old Town Scottsdale or Arcadia gives guests a choice that pairs with a meal.
  • Golf clubs. A cold can at the turn, from the clubhouse in Scottsdale to courses out in Mesa and Gilbert.
  • Hotels and offices. A welcome cooler or a break room fridge that has more than one kind of water.
  • Specialty markets and boutiques. A local brand from Scottsdale on the shelf next to the still cans.

If you are a cafe weighing this, our piece on adding a premium water without adding work walks through how wholesale fits into a busy counter.

Founder-led, same as still

ROCK is founder-led. Brett Yanofsky still sells to and delivers for local accounts across the Phoenix area himself, and sparkling will be no different. When the first batch is ready, he wants to know the businesses pouring it, hear what their guests say and fix anything that needs fixing. You can read more about why he started ROCK in the founder story.

The idea behind the brand stays the same with or without bubbles: "Nothing is more basic to the quality of life than the quality of the water you drink." And 1% of sales still goes to environmental causes through 1% for the Planet.

If your business wants a spot in the first sparkling batch, start on the wholesale page or text Brett directly. We will take it from there.
