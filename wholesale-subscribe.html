No. You can order as little as 1 case.

Yes. Skip a month, change how many cases, pause, or cancel anytime from your account.

Do you deliver to offices?

Yes. Cases ship free to your office address. Skip any month the team is out.

Your first delivery arrives 3 to 5 business days after your order confirms. Delivery is always free.
